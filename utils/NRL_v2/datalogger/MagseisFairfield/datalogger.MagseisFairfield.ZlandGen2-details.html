Below is some configuration-specific information to help you locate your instrument response. ### DC Filter The ZLand Node has an optional Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) low cut filter whose low-frequency corner can be set to 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 or 10 Hz to remove DC offsets, or Off. The responses given here include the 1 Hz and Off options. If you have chosen another setting for this filter, you will need to choose the 1 Hz response and replace the pole value for this stage with 2*pi*fc where fc is your selected corner frequency.
