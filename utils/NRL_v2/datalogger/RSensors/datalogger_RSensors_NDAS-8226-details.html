Below is some configuration-specific information to help you locate your instrument response. ### Main Sample Rate The NDAS-8226 has selectable main sample rates of 1, 10, 50, 100, 125, 250 500 and 1000 Hz. ### Decimated Sample Rate In addition to the main sample rate, the NDAS-8226 produce a 1 Hz sample rate decimated from the main sample rate. To choose responses for these decimated sample rates, select the Main (nondecimated) Sample Rate and Decimated Sample Rate of 1Hz.
