Below is some configuration-specific information to help you locate your instrument response. ### FIR Filters The responses given here are a subset of possible Q412x responses. The FIR cascade for the Quanterra 4120 is user configurable - the user can vary the filter sequence. Gains are user configurable as well. The responses given here represent the **default** cascade described by the following macro: `STAGE=1,2000,0.001663,>>%CHAN_NAMES%` `STAGE=2,400,FS2D5M,<1,>>%CHAN_NAMES%` `STAGE=3,200,F96CM,<2,>>%CHAN_NAMES%` `STAGE=4,100,F96CM,<3,>%CHAN_NAMES%,>>%CHAN_NAMES%` `STAGE=5,40,FS2D5,<3,>%CHAN_NAMES%` `STAGE=6,20,FS2D5,<4,>%CHAN_NAMES%,>>%CHAN_NAMES%` `STAGE=7,10,F96C,<6%M10_OUT%,>>%CHAN_NAMES%` `%MFLAG%STAGE=8,5,F96C,<7,>%CHAN_NAMES%` `STAGE=9,1,F260,<7,>%CHAN_NAMES%` The 0.1 and 0.01 Hz sample rates apply the Quanterra VLP and ULP fir filters to the default 1 Hz sample rate described in the macro.
