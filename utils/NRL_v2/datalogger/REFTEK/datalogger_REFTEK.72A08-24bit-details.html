Below is some configuration-specific information to help you locate your instrument response. ### Configured Sample Rates To optimize throughput, response cascades for the 24-bit 72A08 depended on the choice of simultaneous sample rates configured. This parameter is listed as a hyphen-separated list of sample rates appearing from low to high. Since the throughput limit is estimated to be around 3000 samples per second summed for all channels of the datalogger, responses for sample rate combinations exceeding this have not been included.
