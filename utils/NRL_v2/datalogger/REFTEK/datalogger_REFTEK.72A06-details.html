Below is some configuration-specific information to help you locate your instrument response. ### Preamp Gain Options As a 6-channel recorder, the 72A06 offered preamp gain options of 1 and 8 for channel inputs 1-3 and 32, 128, 512, 2048 and 8196 for channel inputs 4-6. ### Word Size The 72A06 offered a choice of 16 or 32 bits for the output word size. This choice affects the total gain of the datalogger.
