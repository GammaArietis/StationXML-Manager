These responses assume a damping factor of 0.707 and natural frequency of 1 Hz. For other damping factors or natural frequencies, the poles need to be recalculated as follows: > p1, p2 = (-2 * pi * f0 * bt) +/- j * (2 * pi * f0 * sqrt(1 - bt\*\*2) ) where - p1, p2 = poles - f0 = natural frequency - bt = damping factor
